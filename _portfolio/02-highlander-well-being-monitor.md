[Highlander Well-being Monitor](/hwm/): a read-only dashboard developed for UC Riverside's Health, Well-being & Safety research team.

The dashboard helps researchers explore aggregate discussion trends across public Reddit posts: sentiment, recurring themes, HWS classifications, and mapped campus locations. It intentionally excludes Reddit usernames and presents a static snapshot, so data collection, classification, and report generation cannot be triggered from the published site.

It includes interactive charts, a post explorer, a campus map, and a generated community-listening report. The project supports research into student needs while keeping the public presentation scoped to de-identified, aggregate signals.
