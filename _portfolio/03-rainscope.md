[Open RainScope India](/weather/), an interactive weather prototype for exploring forecast precipitation across India.

Search for a city, region, or PIN code—or select a coordinate and radius directly on the map—to see an animated Open-Meteo forecast. The interface shows rain chance, predicted coverage, expected and peak intensity, and accumulated rainfall as the timeline advances.

Built as a focused map-based experience, RainScope also offers adjustable coverage thresholds and a high-contrast rain layer for clearer inspection of changing weather conditions.
